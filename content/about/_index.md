## Your Friendly Neighborhood Navigator

I straddle the line of a roboticist, technical leader, and chief of staff (executive 'random thing do-er'). I seek out hard problems and stumble into opportunities in all domains. I'm an independent, systems, and strategic thinker who's thinking about robotics and coalition building. All of that is to say, I'm just your friendly neighborhood navigator, missing a funny hat.

## Previously

**University of Illinois Department of Aerospace Engineering** - Recent Alumni Board Member

<div class="role-desc"><p>Advised on curriculum relevance, mentored students, and helped strengthen connections between the department and the aerospace and robotics industries.</p></div>

**Samsung Research** - Senior Technical Lead, Robotics Engineering Lead

<div class="role-desc"><p>Chaired the Navigation Working Group and developed Nav2 into a premier community resource. Advocated for high-quality open-source robotics software to democratize access to robotics, with the goal of making Nav2 the "standard library" of robotics.</p></div>
